# Example: The Little Robot and the Last Flower

A finished film made with this pipeline. `Film.tsx` here is the complete source of the film (it was built before the template existed, inside a larger Remotion project, so it has its own helpers, camera and subtitles and some raw frame numbers. Study its drawing, light and motion; for structure, follow the template and SKILL.md).

## Story (as sent to ElevenLabs)
1. In a city made of steel and smoke, nothing green had grown for a hundred years.
2. Every night, a tiny cleaning robot called Pip swept the empty streets.
3. One morning, Pip found something he had never seen before... a little red flower, growing through a crack.
4. He didn't know what it was. But he knew it was alive.
5. Then... the storm came.
6. Pip held his broom over the flower... and stood there, all night long.
7. By morning, his battery was almost gone. But the flower was still standing.
8. And now... it was full of seeds.
9. So Pip did what he did best. He swept... and swept... every seed into every crack in the city.
10. Then spring came. And one by one, the cracks turned green.
11. The little robot who cleaned the city... had brought it back to life.

- **Transformation:** a grey, dead city → a green one. Pip goes from an unnoticed sweeper to the one who regrew the city.
- **Tokens:** the **broom** (a cleaning tool → an umbrella → a planting tool) and the **red flower** (the only colour → the seed of everything → it blooms again at the end).
